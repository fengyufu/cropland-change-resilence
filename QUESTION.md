# Research Question

## Phenomenon

Cropland expansion and cropland reduction may not have equal and opposite effects on ecosystem resilience.

## Working question

Are ecosystem-resilience responses to cropland gain and cropland loss asymmetric under comparable initial cropland fractions and environmental backgrounds?

## Why it matters

A gain/loss asymmetry would mean that removing cropland does not necessarily reverse the resilience effect associated with previous cropland expansion.

## Closest methodological references

- Liu et al. (2025): resilience from vegetation temporal autocorrelation and local stable controls.
- Ma et al. (2026): local matched controls and symmetric gain–loss transitions.

## Proposed distinction

Apply the gain/loss asymmetry framework to cropland fraction change, with explicit comparison of opposite transitions such as 20%→40% and 40%→20%.

## Open questions

- Which cropland product should be the main dataset?
- Is 2000–2020 the final temporal window?
- How should stable controls be defined?
- Which vegetation state variable and resilience metric will be primary?
- Should land-conversion source/destination be part of the main analysis or a later mechanism analysis?
